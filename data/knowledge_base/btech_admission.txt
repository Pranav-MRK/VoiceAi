
B.Tech Admission — Required Documents

Students applying for B.Tech admission must submit the following documents:

1. Government-issued identity proof.
2. Recent passport-size photograph.
3. Class 10 marksheet.
4. Class 12 marksheet.
5. Transfer certificate, if applicable.
6. Migration certificate, if applicable.
7. Caste certificate, if applicable.
8. Income certificate, if applying for an income-based scholarship.
9. Domicile certificate, if required by the admission category.

All documents should be clear and readable. Students should verify the latest admission notice for the final list of required documents.

For technical problems while submitting documents through the online admission portal, students should contact the college admission support team.


B.Tech Admission — Application Process

Students must complete the online application form through the designated admission portal.

The application process includes:

1. Registering on the admission portal.
2. Entering personal and academic information.
3. Uploading the required documents.
4. Reviewing the application.
5. Submitting the application.
6. Completing the applicable fee payment.

Students should verify all information before final submission.

After submission, students should keep their application or registration number for future reference.


B.Tech Admission — Fee Information

The admission fee structure depends on the selected course and admission category.

Students should refer to the latest official fee notice for the exact tuition fee, examination fee, and other applicable charges.

Fees must be paid through the payment methods specified by the college.

For questions about a specific fee payment or transaction, students should contact the admission or accounts support team.


Online Admission Portal — Technical Support

If a student cannot upload a document, they should first verify that the document is clear, readable, and in the format specified by the admission portal.

Students should also check the file size requirements before uploading.

If the problem continues, the student should contact the college admission support team and provide their application number and a description of the issue.

Students should not share passwords or other sensitive account credentials with support staff.
